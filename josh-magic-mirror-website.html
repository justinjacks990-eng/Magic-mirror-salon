About Magic Mirror

Beauty, Done Properly.

Magic Mirror, led by Josh Dreadlocks Guru, is a beauty and professional services business built around one idea: looking good should feel effortless. We serve clients who value quality work, honest communication and a comfortable, welcoming experience.

  • Who we areA client-focused beauty business offering dreadlock care and a growing range of professional services.
  • What we offerDreadlock retouching, reties, styling and maintenance, plus nails, hair and beauty treatments.
